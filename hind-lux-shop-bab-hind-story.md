## Prompt exact

Use case: logo-brand.
Create a completely new, memorable luxury logo for the shop "Hind Lux Shop".
Creative concept: "Bab Hind" (concept name only, DO NOT print it). A gateway to discovering Moroccan craftsmanship, inspired by the welcoming architectural thresholds of Moroccan riads and the arches and jade-colored zellige of Fez gateways. This is a contemporary brand symbol, not a reproduction of a historic monument.

Main emblem: invent a strong compact architectural monogram where a recognizable capital H and a Moroccan horseshoe doorway are ONE ingenious shape. The overall silhouette is a broad, softly rounded horseshoe portal with thick upright jambs. Use elegant ivory negative space and two stylized door leaves to reveal a clearly readable H inside the portal. The H must be structurally integrated, not a typed H pasted inside an arch. Give it sculptural, balanced geometry that could be cast as a beautiful brass shop sign or stamped onto a small label. The arch curves inward slightly at its springing points, characteristic of a horseshoe opening, with a clean square base. Use a rich deep jade green main silhouette and two very small muted-brass square door studs as the only gold accents. Strong silhouette, generous internal openings, precise symmetrical balance, crisp simple geometry, an original professionally resolved mark. The symbol should feel inviting and noble, not religious.

Typography below: exact brand name "HIND" in medium-sized contemporary refined Roman capitals, generous deliberate spacing, with "LUX SHOP" in a smaller very clean sans-serif line beneath. Perfect spelling, optical kerning and centered alignment. The emblem is the hero and clearly separate above the compact wordmark. This must be a markedly different identity from a large flowing Hind script wordmark or gold serif HL initials.

Palette: deep jade green #164B44, warm opaque ivory #F5F0E5, tiny muted brass #B28A4C details. Flat ink colors, no gradients or texture. Keep all type jade.
Composition: one centered square logo presentation on a fully solid opaque ivory background. Emblem in upper center, compact brand name beneath, complete lockup occupies about 68% of canvas height. Considered negative space, premium boutique branding.
Output: one polished finished logo, crisp vector-like flat image, background fully opaque with alpha 255 everywhere. No transparency.
Avoid: literal building illustration, scene, courtyard photo, mosque silhouette, minarets, crescents, stars, flower motifs, shopping bags, crowns, intricate mosaic filling, enclosing circular badge, thin ornamental filigree, 3D, shadows, mockups, paper texture, extra words, Arabic text, collage, watermark. Do not add a tagline or print the concept name.
